Know the fair price before you pay.

New to a city? Sellers and drivers often quote newcomers a higher price. PriceMoja shows what transport, groceries and market goods should cost, and where each price comes from.

What we do

Show fair price ranges

Look up transport fares, groceries and market goods and services, and see the range people normally pay.

Show where prices come from

Every price is labelled: official PPRA reference prices or prices shared and confirmed by the community.

Let you share what you paid

Sign up to add a price, confirm other people's prices, and help the next newcomer.

See at a glance if a quote is fair

Each price comes with a fair range. If a quote is far above it, that is your cue to negotiate.
